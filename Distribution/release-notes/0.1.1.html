Cassini talk 0.1.1

Recording details now tells you more about what was recorded.

  • Meetings recorded on your Mac list their sources in Recording details: your microphone and this computer’s sound, with how much of each was recorded. A source with very little audio is pointed out.
  • When a source was recorded but nobody spoke, Recording details says that no speech was heard.
  • If this computer’s sound was recorded but stayed completely silent, Recording details suggests checking that Cassini can record it, with a button that opens System Settings.
  • If writing a meeting down stops partway, the unfinished words are no longer shown as the meeting’s transcript. Try again starts afresh.
  • Browser recordings that recover from an interruption no longer gain an empty extra source.
