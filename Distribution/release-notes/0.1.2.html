Cassini talk 0.1.2

Read and listen to meetings beside your Library, work with several meetings at once, and keep more control over recording and writing down.

Highlights

  • Select a meeting to read it beside your Library. Press ⌘F to find words in that meeting; playback controls stay within reach as you scroll.
  • Import several meetings or a zip at once, including FLAC audio, and export selected meetings together from the Library.
  • Listen to one speaker at a time, replay a turn or jump to a passage in the transcript. Name speakers, clear a name or merge two speakers, with Undo merge in More actions.
  • Stop writing a meeting down from the Library preview or meeting toolbar without losing its audio or partial words, and resume whenever you’re ready.

Before you update

  • Update the browser companion in Settings to use automatic recording with more browser calls. Keeping the old companion leaves those improvements unavailable.
  • Download the updated Parakeet Ultra in Settings. New meetings use Whistle until it is ready.

Improvements

  • See recent sound and quiet stretches while you record. Messages about silent or interrupted recordings explain what happened and what you can try.
  • The Library and Search keep the search field at the top, put Import Audio… beside Record, and show clear feedback when you drag files onto your meetings.
  • Library and meeting screens share a refreshed colour palette and clearer status icons. Speakers gain consistent colours when you name them, and their names stay consistent across the transcript and playback controls.
  • Short overlapping replies are easier to follow in meetings, Search and exports.
  • Imported meetings show an Imported badge and identify transcripts from another device. A brief confirmation replaces the persistent source-folder card when you first import a meeting.
  • Browser recordings can recover after a page reload.

Fixes

  • Browser call recordings respect your meeting microphone mute.
  • Cassini stays more responsive while recording. Clicking Stop shows Saving… right away in the toolbar and menu bar until the recording finishes saving.
  • Meetings in your Library, Search and Quick Look use their recording date rather than the date the file was saved or imported. New recordings also keep their recording date when shared with another Cassini app.
